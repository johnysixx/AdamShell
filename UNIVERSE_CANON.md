# Universe Canon

## Status

This document is the canonical architectural constitution of the Universe system.

The codebase must conform to this document.

This document is not derived from the current implementation.

The implementation is evaluated against this document.

---

# Prologue

Universe is not Reality.

Universe is not Existence.

Universe is the system through which Existence may be expressed, structured, observed, and preserved.

---

# Book 0 — Universe

## Definition

Universe is the highest architectural container of the system.

It enables Existence to become structured into Reality, Worlds, Layers, Entities, Events, and History.

Universe does not determine outcomes.

Universe provides the conditions under which outcomes may become possible, actualized, observed, and preserved.

## Axioms

1. Universe precedes every internal architectural domain.

2. Universe contains Existence but is not identical to Existence.

3. Universe enables Reality but is not itself Reality.

4. Universe may contain multiple Worlds.

5. Universe may observe, record, and preserve events without directly determining their outcomes.

6. Every architectural component belongs to exactly one defined level of the Canon.

## Consequences

- Reality must not be used as a synonym for Universe.
- World must not be used as a synonym for Universe.
- Layer must not be used as a synonym for World.
- Implementation modules must declare which canonical level they represent.
