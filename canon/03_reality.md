# Book III — Reality

## Definition

Reality is a process.

Reality is not an object.

Reality is not a place.

Reality continuously transforms Truth into observable experience.

Reality does not create Truth.

Reality processes Truth.

Multiple Reality Processes may coexist simultaneously within the same Universe.

## Axioms

### R.1

Reality is a continuous process.

### R.2

Reality never stops while the corresponding World exists.

### R.3

Reality continuously processes Truth.

### R.4

Reality may produce different valid representations of the same Truth.

### R.5

Reality belongs to Worlds.

Reality is not identical to a World.

A World hosts one or more Reality Processes.

### R.6

Every Reality Process follows the Laws of its Reality.

### R.7

Different Worlds may execute different Reality Processes.

### R.8

Reality is independent of any single Observer.

### R.9

Observers participate in Reality.

Observers do not define Reality.

### R.10

Subjective Reality is produced by an Observer processing Truth.

### R.11

Objective Reality is produced independently of any single Observer.

### R.12

Collective Reality emerges from interacting Subjective Realities.

### R.13

Reality may change while Truth remains unchanged.

### R.14

Reality may evolve without violating the Canon.

## Consequences

### R.14.1

Reality is implemented as processes rather than static structures.

### R.14.2

Different Reality Processes may legitimately disagree.

### R.14.3

Disagreement between Reality Processes does not imply contradiction of Truth.

### R.14.4

Reality is responsible for change.

Truth is responsible for consistency.
